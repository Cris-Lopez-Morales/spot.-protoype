# Authored demo data — unchanged from v4.6

32 fictional venues: 24 cafés and 8 bars / clubs. There are 5,000 demo accounts,
610 sample users initially associated with venues, and 60 friends (42 initially
sharing their demo venue). These are authored fixture choices, not verified
Lincoln business counts, exact building locations, total occupancy, or live data.

The regional map, 600-meter minimum separation between authored venue positions,
40 built-in search destinations, and deterministic population allocation are
unchanged by v4.7. After observations expire, activity becomes unknown, not empty.
